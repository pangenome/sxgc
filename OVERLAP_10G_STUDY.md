# Ten-hour overlap scaling study

**Decision window:** 2026-11-24 04:49–14:49 UTC on the rehearsal host. The operator conditionally authorizes the 100 GB rung, not the Pile. This is a measurement plan; a running process, prepared hash microbenchmark, or lower-bound model is **not** a product result. See [100 GB contract](OVERLAP_100G_CONTRACT.md) and [small exact v10 rehearsal](OVERLAP_V10_FIXTURE.md).

1. Finish the already-running `02da5de` **serial 10 GB raw control**. Verify independent ranks, all 10,000,000,001 tagged SA/LCP/BWT rows including positions above u32, and whole reference v8 SHA and all nine members. Bank `goals.json` even on a score miss. No competing scale job. The old pre-monitor 256 MiB stop (`private-pipeline-physical-1795447429450877172`, `run-u137.service`) is not useful construction: after the control's launcher exits, its declared stop and exact two PIDs are checked, then its private cgroup alone is frozen, **not killed**. No spoofed credit or deletion. Report this background difference when comparing historical and new arms.
2. The queued **three complete cold 1 GB products** isolate lanes and batching. All arms in a matched series use one pinned clean private source checkout, identical executables, cgroup grants and cold policy. A verified performance miss keeps its timing and is not a correctness failure.
3. `bash gates/overlap_ten_series.sh SERIAL10 PAIR1 DEADLINE_UNIX` pins the harness, runs the [dictionary grid](gates/overlap_ten_dict_grid.md) **1→4→2** on real retained 10 GB state in one 200 GiB, four-CPU, no-swap cgroup (140 GiB arrays); each full i64 dictionary SA gets an independent post-clock digest; no byte SA file. Pick the **fewest** threads within 5% of fastest sort with no extra sort read/write bytes vs 1. The kernel cannot stop inside one native invocation, so require a 4,200 s admission window; poll stop at boundaries; no signals to the native grid.
4. Rehearse the separate [segmented lexical front](gates/overlap_segmented_studies.md) on 1 GB between the grid and builds (192 MiB/one CPU front, separately metered 3 GiB/one CPU decoder). Neither its IO nor its wall time is a product input or timed concurrently. A refusal blocks only that front's 10 GB qualification. Then run the [full 10 GB product](gates/overlap_ten_matched.md) at **1, 2 and 4 emission lanes**. Start at the 1 GB winner (two if the 1 GB emitter failed its speed test), then the other parallel setting, then one. Keep chosen dictionary threads and batch setting constant (batch only if 1 GB batch score passes). All three enable private reclamation of dead slice markers, proven on a [small matched tmpfs study](gates/receipts/sxls-reclaim-tmpfs/README.md). A 10 GB observation at ~8.6G emitted rows found 65,670 `.done`/`.consumed` names even with bounded payloads; the controller alone had used ~5,570 CPU-s. That predicts scan work, not a wall saving. Never patch frozen finish. Use one pinned OpenMP-capable worker build (`8513ccc`); compare hashes, CPU masks, knobs and verified products. Two finish threads, four shared build CPUs, separate 14,000 MiB/one CPU oracle; count the bitmap, row credits, pieces/slices and packed columns in simultaneous scratch ≤20,000,000,000 B. Report cgroup peak/CPU and process IO; no-swap tmpfs is not free space. Guards are upper limits, not targets.
5. `overlap_scaling_policy.py` records historical combined comparisons (different worker revisions; **no single-knob attribution**) and same-worker, same-harness single-lane-count comparisons. Use actual raw/prepared walls, including oracle backpressure but excluding standalone certification. A default candidate must (a) qualify independently on structure, resource plan, SHA/rows and IO; (b) improve serial 10 GB raw wall by ≥5% with post-front read/write bytes ≤1.05× control; (c) remain <3×/byte vs both banked references and the fastest verified new 1 GB arm at matching endpoints; goal <2×. Of these, within 3% of fastest raw wall prefer lower total build cgroup CPU, then the smallest lane count within 3% of that CPU. Publish the actual profile and command when the sweep completes. This is **best measured on this grant**, not a universal optimum. Do not change defaults from component-only results.
6. Only promote after banking auditable receipts on `origin/main`. No larger launch from a dirty tree or a local-only commit. Independently qualify width, RAM, 2N scratch, cold private inputs, row oracle, v10 map/tap/append, and deadline for the proposed 100 GB stage. A 100 GB front/census and a 100 GB certified product are different. If the full-rung ETA exceeds the remaining window, do not silently start an unbounded build. Continue bounded integration, diagnosis or rehearsal instead.

Preserve the empty maps and traceback. The exact digest-bound first arm uses native effect checks plus the audited launch source, not retroactively invented `/proc` data. Postcheck `248d280` resumes only 2 and 1 lanes, on the **unchanged** `0769803` timed harness and `8513ccc` tools; a separate stable allowlisted `/proc` observation is required on each remaining arm. The partial score is not a default or an admission for 100 GB.

## Serial control: certified, speed target missed against 1 GB

[`packed-serial-10g`](gates/receipts/packed-serial-10g/) (`proc_98da`, worker source `02da5de`): **10,198.880 s raw (2 h 49 min 59 s), 9,824.947 s prepared**. Verified 318,164,488 parse ranks, every 10,000,000,001 raw row (5,705,032,705 positions > u32, max LCP 699,594), and byte-identical nine-member **9,560,930,192 B** product, SHA `26cfb2de0cccab8a8712a332956d58693dcbbb34373a9031a7bbaaa08cc504c9`. Front 369.847 s, native dictionary sort 783.598 s with no disk traffic, emit 8,581.046 s including blocked/write time to sink 2,790.914 s and audit 1,051.665 s. Sink/finish overlap those times; do not add them. Raw worker traffic 34.044 GB read / 48.637 GB written; post-front 23.854 / 17.763 GB. Verifier separate: 1,330.11 CPU-s and 10.0 GB reads. Shared 124.962 GiB / 200 GiB peak, no swap/OOM; 15,090,631,800 B peak counted scratch / 20 GB; whole directory allocated 41,003,700,224 B including input copies and product.

Normalized raw/prepared time per byte: **2.229×/2.299×** vs packed 1 GB, **1.834×/1.873×** vs 3 GB. IO and <3× promotion floor passed; **not** the desired <2× against both. Scalar sink issued 424,823,569 write-family calls for just 14,413,824 attributed write bytes. About 7,246 CPU-s of the build cgroup are *not accounted for by the front + three workers*; this difference is not a directly measured Python-only timer. Both effects motivate the matched studies.
