# Returning a disk slice does not free the finish's LCP arrays

The [five-slot writer](overlap_v8_sxls.md) bounds payload inodes and its own codec arrays. The non-spooling frozen v8 `LcpColumn::openNextSlice` copies the payload and escape pairs, closes/unlinks the SXLS and **then** publishes `.consumed`. `loadBands` opens all slices covering the requested fold span **before** dropping passed private copies. Its `max_live_lcp_slice_bytes` is a logical diagnostic, neither a process RSS peak nor the sum of all LCP allocations. A copied disk slice and the staged band can hold the same data simultaneously.

## Predict and fence the diagnostic before exposing its inputs

For this exact seam-free maximal-run stream, the source and normalized coalescers each delay one run. Let `y[i]` be the first row rank of run `i`, with `y[R]=n`. After receiving a piece ending at exclusive run `r`, the fold has emitted up to row `y[max(0,r−2)]` (exclusive). Ignore empty loads. At EOF first dispatch the last piece this way, then `finishStream()` drains the pending tail up to `n`. Each new load with end `end` includes the preceding last rank: `[max(0,previous_end−1),end)`. No such formula is asserted for fragmented equal runs, seams, spooling, repair chunks or arbitrary cuts.

For a load of length `L`, spanning source slices `i..j` inclusive, the pinned class reports `L + P[j+1] − P[i]`, where `P` is cumulative marker plus escape bytes **without the 48-byte headers**. Keep just the prefix values at slice ends (383 u64s at 100 MB), two pending run starts and scalar counters. The internal piece is published on the first row of the following run; EOF flushes the last slice before the cutter finishes. Check the load at that lookahead/EOF. If `j` is still being built, check its known prefix now (the band can allocate `L` already), then check its *complete* payload before forwarding the slice-closing packet (the reader can then open it). Several folds may end inside that same unfinished slice: retain the largest `L − P[i]` across them, not just the last. If the slice closed first, its immutable prefix totals are already available when the piece closes. A failed check can leave partial files but cannot release the offending packet or certify EOF. This is a new constraint on the fixed relay, not evidence that it has passed a real 100 MB stream.

## Conditional backing-array charge, not scratch or total RAM

Let `H=3,958,321`. Each staged `deep` pair belongs both to the loaded `L` rows and to the unretired source copies. Their markers cover at least `L` rows. Consequently `H ≥ 2L + 16*deep_count ≥ 18*deep_count`, or at most **219,906 pairs**. In the audited single-dispatch `FinishPool` a job has a *fresh* `Band`; all workers share its pointers, and `wait()` clears the prior job before another dispatch. Under the pinned GCC/libstdc++ vector policy, singleton push-backs from empty require at most 262,144 entries. **Charge old plus new during reallocation**: `16*(131072+262144) = 6,291,456 B`, in addition to `H`. The other transient is `loadSliceEscapes`' raw input buffer while resizing the private source vector. Full slices have at most 134,284 escapes at the enforced extent, and the short last slice has only 123,640 rows: at most **2,148,544 B** extra. This occurs before the new job fills `deep`, not concurrently with its reallocation. The two source vectors are resized from empty and moved into the deque, not copied a third time.

Conditional upper: `max(H+2,148,544, H+6,291,456) = 10,249,777 B` of these reader payloads; with two u64 rank arrays for at most 65,536 runs, **11,298,353 B**. This excludes both persistent BandSlots, allocator metadata/retained freed memory, deque and job controls, worker stacks, corpus and PFCK mappings, bitmap, member directories, IO/cache, relay and other services. A different library/lifetime policy needs a new charge. The old 3,958,321 B is not an 11.3 MB RSS measurement, and this calculation allocates no part of the joint grant.
