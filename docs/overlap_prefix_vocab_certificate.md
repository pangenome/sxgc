# How much can a certified shorter vocabulary prove about the actual target?

**[The containment audit passed](../gates/receipts/target-prefix-vocab/).** 10 GB SHA matched exactly; the retained three-column logical upper is 199,592,925,480 B (402,880,168 B left including margin/manifest). 36,475 two-code cells fit 192M descriptor slots, two definitely need finer partitions and 113 are unresolved. Not a physical-fit certificate. This is a prefix-extension test, **not** tenfold extrapolation or a new vocabulary census. The [10 GB bank](../gates/receipts/vocab-10g/) has independently checked distinct word and finite descriptor counts; the [100 GB scan](../gates/receipts/scanplan-100g/) has occurrence counts but no dedup. Similar filenames, document maps, aggregate statistics and equality at a few sampled offsets do **not** prove that one raw text starts with the other. Do not apply the following bounds to the pair until their byte relationship is authenticated.

**Invariant.** With the same local 8-byte trigger, cut zero and every trigger starting at `1..n−8` inclusive, every old ordinary phrase (through the following trigger's full overlap) survives a byte-for-byte right extension. Its key, owned width and all its owned finite offsets survive. The *old EOF word does not*: it is separately tagged, occurs only once, owns its whole length and may be replaced/split. Write `P,m₀,k₀,U₀` for prefix size, occurrences, distinct words including EOF, and distinct owned offsets. There are `m₀−k₀` redundant ordinary occurrences and `P−U₀` redundant owned offsets already present in any such extension. Thus

* `k₀ ≤ k ≤ m−(m₀−k₀)` (old ordinary vocabulary + one *new* EOF at minimum);
* `U₀−old_EOF_len+new_EOF_len ≤ U ≤ N−(P−U₀)`;
* For a two-code cell `c`, `O₀,D₀` are the prefix's weighted rows / distinct descriptors, `O` target weighted rows, and `e₀,e` the old/new unique EOF word's offset-cell histograms. Then `max([O>0],D₀−e₀+e) ≤ D ≤ O−(O₀−D₀)` **per cell**. Use raw byte `x` and `1+next` (0 for END), not the numeric remap of either index. Nonempty cells must have a descriptor. Lower cells sum to a possibly stronger total lower; upper cells sum to the global upper. No need to transfer frequency estimates from a sample.
* Removing the old terminal removes at most its length in finite keys, and at most **one** descriptor per exact finite-key tie (two suffixes of that word starting at different offsets have different lengths). All distinct ordinary descriptors and their equality/inequality as finite byte strings remain. This gives lower, not upper, bounds from the old group count / largest tie. Needing to split a **two-code cell** is not evidence of a too-large indivisible complete-key tie.

**Why authenticate 10 GB at all?** Even the already authenticated 100 MB prefix guarantees only 481,064 duplicate occurrences / 4,565,987 duplicate offsets; that correction doesn't put the *worst-case* retained dictionary + keys + inverse below 200,000,000,000 B. The 10 GB prefix would guarantee **83,691,765 / 870,709,888** respectively. The wide native tape is `T64=U+16k−8`, not the v8 packed tape. If the larger prefix matches, this would move the `T64+16m` upper from **201,802,703,608 to 199,592,925,480 B**, leaving **402,880,168 B** after 4 MiB controls and a 48-byte position manifest. That is a *phase's logical payload ledger*, not an allocated-space proof. Filesystem reserve, metadata, allocator growth, cache, other phases and their concurrency are not in that difference. A builder that actually retires the tape earlier has different lifetimes.

**Falsifiable gate.** A new read-only containment audit, not a lease for the old full scan or product. Compare the SHA of precisely `[0,10,000,000,000)` in the previously identified raw target inode to the independently banked 10 GB SHA. On mismatch refuse; never fill in a missing relationship from provenance alone. Also read exactly 16 bytes at the target EOF for the terminal adjustment; extract the prefix's 80 terminal bytes *during* the prefix hash. Check stable dev/ino/size/mtime/ctime against the pinned full-scan identity before/after; the remainder of the target is **not rehashed**. No original eviction, private corpus copy, row/phrase enumeration, sort, tape read, new column, image or index. No cold timing. One pinned CPU, 192 MiB/no swap, ≤150 s, STOP/deadline and attributed IO limit (`10 GB+192 MiB`), exclusive timing lease, fresh directory, small bounded receipts. Report the two per-cell bound digests, all cells needing subdivision or investigation at **192,000,000** slots, and the more conservative of the allowed encoding dimensions for the rolling model (`U_upper,k_lower`); its epoch count still assumes **every actual range fits**. Different target bytes, trigger, boundary rule, ownership or dictionary encoding invalidate the transfer. This cannot determine exact target `k,U`, schedule a full build or certify runtime.
