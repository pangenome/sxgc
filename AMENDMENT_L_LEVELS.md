# AMENDMENT L (THE LEVELS / THE UNIFORM RECURSION) — spec before dispatch

Status: FROZEN. The parse-of-parse recursion becomes the machine's
uniform law: levels are data, not modes. Plus the 64-bit SA kernel.
Operator order: small scale proves it first; byte-identity at every
anchor must hold with the recursion ON.

## L1 — Levels are data, not modes

The census decides the level count BY NUMBER at every scale (Sawzall
law — no routing decisions, no it-fits special cases). Construction:
parse down while the census says so; build the SA at the deepest
level; induce upward through the phrasebook chain. The same code path
runs at 100 MB (1-2 levels), 100 GB (1-2 levels), and the pile (2
levels). The small-scale arms ARE the recursion's test flights.

## L2 — The level chain

Level k+1's "corpus" is level k's ID sequence — references only, the
no-expansion law holds at every level (no text is ever stored; level
phrasebooks are (first-occurrence position, length) references).
The one-sided trigger parses every level uniformly (the ID alphabet
is a dense integer alphabet; the census tunes the threshold by
number, not by decision). Deeper recursion converges ~30x per level
(measured condensation: 29.86x sandbox, 29.83x 1 GB); the base case
is the census number: the parse fits the SA kernel.

## L3 — Induction upward

The deepest level's SA (small, under the kernel ceiling) induces the
next level's aligned order through the phrasebook chain; the final
induction lands at level 0 — the corpus — exactly as the current
machine induces level-0 unaligned rows locally from level-1 order.
The container members are unchanged: the product is level-0 facts
only.

## L4 — The 64-bit kernel

Vendor libsais64 alongside libsais (Apache-2.0, same author); the
kernel selection is BY NUMBER (M <= 2^31 -> 32-bit; else 64-bit if
within its verified ceiling; else one more parse level — the census
arithmetic decides, never a mode flag). Verify libsais64's exact
ceiling at vendoring time and journal it.

## Non-goals

No container format changes. No new modes — this amendment DELETES
the last scale-dependent special case (the >2^31 fallback) in favor
of the census arithmetic. The B4 scratch law applies at every level
(peak live scratch journaled per level).
