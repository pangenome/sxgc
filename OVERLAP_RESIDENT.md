# One resident PFP comparison (prepared 1 GB lexparse)

Question: is the 96-minute external emission inherent to the order, or the cost of repeatedly reconstructing local working sets? Hold the text, overlap convention, and row/finish oracles fixed. Give the build 20 GiB shared (no swap, 4 CPU quota, 2 finish threads); reserve 14 GiB for resident producer arrays. Keep the old external gate unchanged. Falsifiable prediction: both sorts fit separately, emission needs **zero dictionary scans from disk**, and elapsed time from the prepared IDs/refs through the 1 GB container is less than the old *emission alone*. No implication that a signed-32-bit kernel or these density ratios work at 10 GB or the Pile.

* Input: only `parse.u32` (m IDs), `dictionary.refs` (k position/length/EOF pairs), corpus, remap. No precomputed order, alignments, lists, keys or windows. A **check-only** private copy of `inverse.u32` verifies each rank; no value from it participates in construction. `libsais_int` takes the flat m integers (not the text, and no second parse at this rung). Give it 6k extra i32 slots in its SA buffer per its workspace contract, then free them.
* Read the corpus sequentially into an anonymous byte array. Encode used raw bytes in monotone byte codes 2..255 (explicit refusal if not enough codes); dictionary = each distinct word once, with code 1 for virtual END only on the final word, 0 separator after each word. For `z` identical phrase IDs, byte LCP at a parse boundary = displacement across those `z` strides + finite LCP at the first unequal word. Get `z` from the integer PLCP. Build occurrence lists in successor-SA order, with separate text position, next rank and external predecessor. Drop corpus, parse and parse SA.
* `libsais` sorts the D dictionary **byte positions** once. There is no raw corpus SA/PLCP column at any point. A u32 owner map discards separator/overlap-only positions. Compare consecutive surviving finite tails directly in resident bytes, not their concatenated suffixes beyond the separator. Different boundary-complete tails are prefix-free; equal tails are merged on next rank, with byte LCP `len - 8 + aligned_RMQ`. Long/skewed words are a counterexample to treating direct LCP comparisons as constant cost: the sum of finite key lengths bounds the bytes matched (40.925 GB of *logical* keys on this rung), not the physical IO. No copies of those keys. Equal keys have at most one offset per word; the plan includes space even for a doubled heap of 2k cursors.
* Account for overlapping lifetimes, not summed phase peaks. Parse bound `8m + 8k + 24k` bytes including refs/workspace; packing/list bound `N + D + 20k + 32m`. During sort, `5D` bytes for dictionary and SA; during emission `9D` including owner. Both retain offsets/CSR/RMQ (~`8k + 13.5m`), with explicit allowance for library work and the merge heap. Refuse if the maximum plan exceeds the producer grant. Kernel uses signed 32-bit lengths, output u32 triples; n, m, k and D checked before sorts. The sandbox fixture paths have **not** had their caps raised.
* Stream LE triples to the Rust piece/LCP cutter and a separately capped raw verifier. No triples file. Start sink/finish at emission; times overlap. Measure `read_bytes/write_bytes` per worker and producer phase, RSS (own VmHWM reset per phase), shared memory peak, cgroup IO and time blocked writing both pipes. The run starts after lexical parsing; copies/eviction, oracle work and post-run hash are separate. Frozen finish in its usual v8 nine-member profile. Scratch plan reserves the retained IDs/refs, sink backlog, fixed-grid PFCK/bitmap and the known profile's finish spools before emitting. Count actual scratch including nested pieces/slices; distinguish sampled whole-directory allocated disk, input/check copies and the partial/final product.

Reproduce: `bash gates/overlap_resident_gate.sh`. First `gates/overlap_resident_test.py` compares every triple on 18 small inputs including NUL, EOF at a trigger, ties and repetitive text; checks wrong-rank, byte-alphabet and undersized-grant refusals and stop=75. The measured arm uses a new directory and private cold inodes (`mincore` zero), never evicts shared files. On a failure keep the receipt; do not advance the rung.
