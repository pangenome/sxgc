# Random byte lookup in a resident lexical dictionary: pay for direct literal offsets?

**Small lookup component only.** The original [block-16 lexical codec](LEXICAL_BLOCKS.md) rebuilds a view from the restart before answering a query. That's reasonable when a view is reused for many bytes, not obviously so for the scattered `(word,offset+depth)` probes of a local radix order. The measured 10 GB wire size is not a speed measurement. Do not turn a lookup ratio into a producer or IO projection. `overlap_lex_direct.rs` has a 10,000,000-byte fixture-only builder and no large payload loader or product routing.

**Measured:** the [archived lookup study](../../gates/receipts/lex-lookup-small/) verified every expected symbol. Worse direct/restart scatter wall ratios **0.174 / 0.166 / 0.476** on book/wide/deep; direct/flat **6.732 / 4.494 / 9.859**. On the deep sequential scan direct was 1.812× restart despite the same literal data. This passes the scatter hypothesis, not a flat speed target or a scale gate. Replay receipts, not the timed suite.

## Invariant and cost

Keep the **same literal suffixes**: at restart, take `p[0]=0`; otherwise `p[i]=lcp(raw[i-1],raw[i])`. Let `l[i]=len[i]-p[i]` and `e[i]=sum(l[0..=i])`, with `e[-1]=0`. Each block stores one width byte, `p[1..n]`, `e[0..n]`, then the concatenated literals (`n<=16`, widths chosen independently per block, unsigned little endian). Use the shortest whole-byte width that fits each maximum, at least one byte. One u64 offset per block plus a final offset; charge two u64 words `k,eof_id` in the direct wire. Assume complete, unique, sorted `(raw,EOF)` records and exactly one tagged record. A tagged duplicate of ordinary raw bytes may have a zero-length literal but not an empty owned stride. No byte value is reserved in the raw data.

* Length at slot `i` is `p[i]+e[i]-e[i-1]`. Out-of-range is absent, except that exactly `pos=len` in the unique EOF word yields explicit END=0. A real byte maps to `1+raw` (including raw 0 and 255).
* When `pos < p[i]`, the requested byte is in the predecessor at the **same position**. Step left until the nearest `j<=i` with `p[j]<=pos` (the restart guarantees termination). Return `literal[e[j-1]+pos-p[j]]`. The prefix invariant proves this is the exact original byte; no full word, view list, or payload copy. At most 15 hops, 0 on a current literal. Ordinary owned length is `len-8`; EOF owns `len`. There is no change to virtual END ordering or the separate global END row.
* A long shared prefix is a **counterexample** to universal one-step lookup. In a full 16-word block sharing the first byte, looking up that byte uniformly takes `(0+...+15)/16 = 7.5` predecessor hops. Reading all bytes of a word amortizes a conventional multi-slice restart view. Neither hop counts nor header counts are cache misses, disk bytes, or phase time.

**Space bound without guessing header distributions.** Write `q=ceil(k/16)`, `L=sum(E_b)` where `E_b` is the total literal payload of block b. The minimum direct wire is `16+8(q+1)+q+L+(2k-q)`. For each threshold `t=256,65536,16777216`, at most `min(q,floor(L/t))` blocks use another byte per cumulative-end field, and none if `max E_b<t`. A word's recovered literal addresses are strictly increasing within its block (truncate a prefix, append a later literal). Hence `p<=len<=E_b`; the same qualifying blocks bound the prefix-width increments, and none if `max_word<t`. Charge at most 16 ends and 15 prefixes per qualifying block, capped by their global counts. This bounds **this representation**, not allocator, scratch for loading or elapsed time.

`python3 -BE gates/overlap_lex_direct_plan.py` replays the public small receipts, no 10 GB tape read. On the measured `k=234,472,723`, `L=7,674,858,399`, `max E_b <= 89,135` (largest original complete block), direct wire is between **8,261,040,237 and 8,718,961,516 B** vs **8,278,299,754 B** measured ULEB wire: at most **1.053231×**, not a measured new size. At **10× k and L, unchanged maxima and previous 10× navigation model only**, the upper core before ordering is 120,063,436,080 B, leaving 30,260,419,280 B against a hypothetical 140 GiB comparison. No actual 100 GB `k`, grant, bulk builder, integrated emitter, oracle or v10 decision follows.

## Falsifiable small lookup study (fixed before launch)

`gates/overlap_lex_lookup.{rs,py,sh}`: 64 disjoint 3,072-byte windows of pinned `pg64317.txt`, using only ordinary phrases whose two FNV triggers are internal; one synthetic EOF. Separate seeded dictionaries: 32,768 wide words of 9..105 bytes, and 1,024 families of 16 120-byte words sharing their first 96 bytes within each block. Exactly one tagged EOF per dictionary. No Pile reads or joins between windows. Same flat `u64` offsets + raw adapter, existing ULEB restart view, and direct table, all warm and resident. This is **not** a repetition of the byte/bit sort crossover.

* `scatter`: query one position per **distinct owned descriptor**, offset plus seeded depth 0..16, shuffled. Look up a fresh view per query. This is a proxy, **not** a recorded sorter trace or a working set the size of the Pile.
* `head`: one first-byte query per word in shuffled ID order; for the aligned synthetic families this must take exactly 7.5 direct hops per query. `scan`: lexically visit each word once, reuse its view for all bytes plus the two boundary queries (`len`, `len+1`). These expose prefix skew and view reuse, not just favorable scatter throughput.
* On each fixed trace use flat→restart→direct, then direct→restart→flat. Repeat each trace within its clock to check at least 1,048,576 queries. Exact comparison of **each** returned symbol/absence with an expectation prepared independently from the source word. Report CPU and wall, both repetitions, counts/lengths and wire; no digest-only acceptance. Generation, format construction, expected answers and tracing are outside query clocks. Other process time and IO are separate. One dedicated 192 MiB/one CPU/no-swap service, lease and fixed deadline; no row columns, spool or product. The raw book and sample are pinned, not cold-storage benchmarks.
* Use the **worse** direct/restart scatter wall ratio of the reversed orders. Primary prediction: **≤2/3** on both book and wide, **≤1.10** on deep, direct/restart logical wire **≤1.10** on every shape. Report head/scan and the worse direct/flat ratios separately, including whether **every** trace is <2× and <3× flat. Passing the compressed-adapter comparison is not passing flat proximity, much less a raw/prepared row-stream or product gate. Failure is evidence; no threshold changes or automatic larger dispatch.
