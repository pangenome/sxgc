## What is actually on the critical path?

Keep the verified 10 GB resident builder and the historical tape-built 100 GB index. The point of the alternative is to replace the part that orders suffixes, not the index or its query semantics. At 100 GB the measured 3.18 billion phrase occurrences exceed the old signed sort's length; simply widening the fields doesn't buy the RAM for the old all-at-once sort. The rolling builder ranks overlapping phrases, orders bounded ranges and sends the same `(position, LCP, predecessor)` rows to existing consumers. It remains an experiment until the **whole target product** is practical.

**Do not make the 100 MB v8 file margin a 100 GB v10 prerequisite.** It matters if running that exact v8 arm, whose HOLD stands. But 64 full run pieces occupy 49,285,888 bytes at 27 bits and 59,771,648 at 37 bits (`64 × [44 + 5×65536 + 16×ceil(width×65536/64)]`). That's 49% of the small input vs 0.060% of the target; this particular overhead is almost fixed, not linear in input length. Conversely, the target really has a document-context consumer that v8 doesn't test. The historical `CONTEXT_TRIE_TAP` records **800,000,000,008 logical position bytes**. An overlap relay that writes a *separate* position band would write and read that volume on top of the row stream. Feed the existing Rust trie builder from the row pipe instead. This is not a prediction of device traffic, a 100 GB grant, or a reason to bypass exact row and final-product identity.

The rolling producer, direct Rust trie pipe, row sink, frozen v10 finish, and both document joins have now [built one complete index together](gates/receipts/rolling-v10-product/). All 6,311,446 independently checked rows and the entire 378,392-byte product matched on a deliberately compressible generated text. The reference did not use the new producer or the new trie transport. This settles the **product interface**, not scale or performance. The [subsequent real 100 MB v10 pair](gates/receipts/real-v10-product-100m/) also passed: independent reference and rolling products identical in all 87,192,432 bytes, 1.550× raw→joined wall under the joint physical and logical scratch caps. ## Why multiplying the rung is not an implementation

**Measured**, `gates/receipts/packed-front-10g/front-end.json`: `m=318164488`, `k=234472723`, transformed dictionary `D=11239544612`, 192 MiB/1 CPU/no swap, 357.757 s raw→packed only, 15,090,631,800 counted peak. Separate 3 GB bitmap v8 product: 1,667.955 s raw vs 1,573.783 s prepared with oracle pipe, 4 build CPUs + 1 oracle, 60 GiB + 4200 MiB, no swap. The 10 GB serial v8 product then measured 10,198.880 s raw / 9,824.947 s prepared and 124.962 GiB peak shared. Its **grants**, not measured peaks, are 140 GiB arrays / 200 GiB shared + 14000 MiB oracle, same CPU quotas; front watchdog 1200 s and product watchdog 12822 s. Neither time limit is a speed certificate. Historical 100 GB tape finish alone took 12:14:04 at 552% CPU, peak RSS 33,666,260 KiB; ctx-tap 11:58:54, peak 101,981,180 KiB, overlapped. Rehearsal sampled scratch 117,999,996,546 B excluded final product. These are not overlap wall, peak sums or grants.

**Sensitivity only**, `m,k,D := 10 × measured 10 GB`; corpus changes. Current model (arrays in bytes, equations in script): m=3,181,644,880; k=2,344,727,230; D=112,395,446,120. Parse + `6*k` workspace=17,250,008,260 >2,147,483,647; both row count and dictionary IDs also overflow signed i32. u32 occurrence/rank is only conditional (<2^32, with sentinels/one-past checked). Simply widening dictionary SA or casting IDs cannot work. Require a measured census and validated wide integer SA/PLCP + continuation ranking, or recursive upper sort and exact lower-rank induction/PLCP with checked widths at **every** level. A contraction ratio is not a proof. Exercise forced-small ceilings and compare all ranks/rows across the cut. Rung guards and packed-tape limit are intentional. `lexparse10`'s 1024 × 64 MiB buckets cannot even contain 100 GB of raw record payload across them; redesign splitting for skew/duplicate groups, not just input-size constant.

Projected packed tape=119,429,627,809 B, parse=12,726,579,520 B; phase arrays parse/aux/sort/emit=238,671,876,049 / 487,373,185,888 / 1,109,759,038,076 / 1,231,588,268,556 B. No swap, allocator, file cache, tap, finish, pipe or oracle included; widening parse/adding radix state raises costs. A 64-bit dictionary position fits D, not a dense resident sort grant. Text offsets, prefix sums, LCP, global ranks and byte products require checked wide arithmetic; row43 position/LCP can represent N (<2^43), but the oracle is explicitly 10 GB only.

Alternative to 8D full SA: keep lexically disjoint **exact** ranges, finish each equal finite suffix group together by continuation rank; output globally ordered contiguous row intervals and prove coverage once. Under the optimistic old `overlap_range_fit.py` list/RMQ model alone `D+12(k+1)+13m+8ceil(m/16)` = 183,484,378,772 B, before records, heap, wide RMQ delta, allocator, routing or concurrent consumers. Even this exceeds the 140 GiB array rung. Repeated dictionary passes cost `batches*D` logical visits (not physical reads); nonresident indirect key compares may fault randomly. Measure skew/largest indivisible group incl heap growth, exact per-range routing costs, bounded fan-in and scratch retirement under matched working-set/cache ratios on small corpora. Refuse oversize group unless proven finer ordering or exact bounded merge. No extrapolation of 1 GB's 3340 intervals into a distribution certificate.

The independent [10 GB vocabulary and tape ledger](gates/receipts/vocab-10g/) supplies a better sensitivity than just multiplying packed bytes: at 10×, block-16 lexical bytes plus sampled heads, starts, `Psi`, adjusted PLCP, predecessor and RMQ model **115.657 GB** before ordering. This would leave **34.667 GB** of a hypothetical 140 GiB array comparison, not measured 100 GB headroom. Four-k vs six-k parse workspace alone changes that modeled phase from 138.664 to 176.180 GB. Need integrated lifetimes, actual `k`, codec/access costs, bounded order/merge, consumers and independent verification. The scan did not supply those.
